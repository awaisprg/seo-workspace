The Experience

A quiet ritual, every visit.

From the moment you sit down, every step is unhurried, considered, and built around your hair — not a clock.

i.

Consult

A real conversation about what you love, what you want to change, and what your hair will love.

ii.

Cleanse

A long, slow shampoo and scalp massage at the basin — the part of the visit you'll think about all week.

iii.

Craft

The work itself — cut, colour, style — done with care, no shortcuts, no double-booking.

iv.

Finish

A polished blow-out and a take-home routine you can actually recreate at the bathroom mirror.
