## Top 5 Direct Competitors — Deep Dive

### 1. medcaremso.com (DR 52, 15.6K traffic, +50% over PRG)

**Positioning:** Full RCM + medical billing for multiple specialties.

**What they're doing better than PRG:**
- Higher DR (+11) — more refdomain authority
- 4× organic traffic on similar topical scope
- 2,505 keywords vs PRG's 436 — ~6× content surface

**Inferred strategy:** Programmatic specialty + state matrix. Likely thousands of low-volume long-tail pages.

**How to compete:** Match their breadth on specialty pages, beat them on depth (case studies, named experts, original data). Build "[specialty] medical billing" pages that reference real CPT codes, modifiers, and reimbursement rates — concrete > generic.

### 2. medicalbillersandcoders.com (DR 57, 7.9K traffic)

**Positioning:** Network/marketplace tone — connects practices to billers.

**What they're doing better:**
- Even higher DR (+16) — strongest backlink profile of direct competitors
- Brand suggests they own broad informational queries

**Inferred strategy:** Long-form blog + directory pages. Medium content velocity, strong inbound link earning.

**How to compete:** Don't fight their network/directory framing — PRG is a service provider. Beat them on commercial-intent terms ("medical billing for [specialty]", "outsourced medical billing", state pages).

### 3. curemd.com (DR 69, 30K traffic)

**Positioning:** EHR-first software vendor; medical billing is a secondary line.

**What they're doing better:**
- Highest DR among peers (69)
- 7.6× PRG's organic traffic (mostly EHR-driven)

**How to compete:** Don't chase EHR queries. Stay disciplined on billing-services intent. CureMD's billing pages are weaker than their EHR pages — beatable on focused service-page intent.

### 4. bellmedex.com (DR 51, 10.5K traffic)

**Positioning:** Direct PRG analogue — same size, same services, same audience.

**What they're doing better:**
- Same DR tier, 2.6× traffic
- 1,817 keywords vs PRG's 436

**How to compete:** Head-to-head. Audit their top pages, identify content gaps, replicate + improve. Gap analysis sprint recommended (use Ahrefs `site-explorer-organic-keywords` filtered to bellmedex's URLs PRG doesn't own).

### 5. imedclaims.com (DR 40, 16K traffic)

**Positioning:** Same DR as PRG, 4× the traffic. Pure-play medical billing.

**What they're doing better:**
- Same authority, vastly more content
- 3,556 keywords vs 436 — ~8× content surface

**Implication:** PRG's authority is roughly equal to imedclaims, but content output is 8× lower. The gap is closeable through content production alone — no link-building required.
