## Editorial Standards (apply to every page)

1. **Author byline + bio + headshot** (no anonymous content)
2. **Reviewer byline** for clinical/coding accuracy ("Reviewed by [name, CPC/CPB/CPMA]")
3. **Last-updated date** visible to user
4. **Citations** to primary sources (CMS, AMA, AAPC) with outbound `rel="noopener"` links
5. **Clear extract-friendly structure** — H2/H3 hierarchy, definition boxes, numbered lists, summary blocks at top
6. **One concrete example per major concept** (a real CPT code, a sample EOB, a redacted case)
7. **Internal links** — minimum 3 contextual links to other PRG pages, plus standard hub/footer links
8. **CTA** — soft (download guide) above fold, hard (request quote / book consultation) mid + bottom
9. **Image quality** — no stock-doctor photos; use diagrams, screenshots, real workflow visuals
10. **Mobile-first** — verify on real devices, not just emulator
